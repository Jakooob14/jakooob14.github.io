## Overview

**U Žejdlíků** is a digital presence developed for a traditional Czech restaurant. The website delivers an inviting culinary atmosphere, interactive daily and seasonal menus, opening hours, and reservation options.

## Key Features
- **Dynamic Menu Showcase**: Clear categorization of daily specials, main courses, and beverages with allergen info.
- **Atmospheric Branding**: Elegant imagery capturing the venue's interior and gastronomic offerings.
- **Fast Mobile Experience**: Optimized for guests searching for lunch menus on smartphones on the go.
