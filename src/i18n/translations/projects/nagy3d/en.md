## Overview

**Nagy3D** is a commercial website designed for a specialized 3D printing and additive manufacturing business. It highlights engineering capabilities, material selections, prototype showcases, and rapid quotation inquiry workflows.

## Key Features
- **High-Impact Visuals**: Clean presentation of custom 3D printed components and manufacturing processes.
- **Service Breakdown**: Clear overviews of FDM and SLA technologies, available filaments, and tolerances.
- **Conversion-Optimized Contact Flow**: Intuitive inquiry forms for rapid quote requests.
