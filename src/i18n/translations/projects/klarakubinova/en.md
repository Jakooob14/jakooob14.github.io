## Overview

**Klára Kubínová** is a modern personal branding and professional services website designed for an independent legal counsel and attorney. The site communicates trust, transparency, practice areas, and straightforward consultation booking.

## Key Features
- **Trust-Focused Design**: Refined color palette, clear typography, and professional photography.
- **Practice Areas**: Structured breakdown of legal expertise, corporate advisory, and civil law counsel.
- **Client Onboarding**: Accessible consultation request forms and transparent fee schedules.
