## Overview

**Prémiový Makléř** is an exclusive web platform crafted for a premier real estate broker. It presents high-value property listings, market insights, client testimonials, and a sophisticated lead acquisition funnel.

## Key Features
- **Luxury Aesthetic**: Minimalist, typography-driven layout conveying authority and professionalism.
- **Detailed Property Spotlights**: High-resolution gallery presentation, floor plans, and localized neighborhood amenities.
- **Valuation Inquiries**: Smooth multi-step contact flows for property owners seeking valuation.
